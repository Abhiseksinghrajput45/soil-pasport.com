FAQ

SPX Power™ questions, answered.

What is SPX Power™?

SPX Power™ converts agricultural biomass into electricity through combustion or gasification, producing a distributed power source for on-site use, local grids or rural electrification.

What feedstocks does SPX Power™ use?

Biomass and agricultural or process residue, converted through combustion or gasification power-generation technology.

Where is SPX Power™ best suited?

Distributed and rural electricity generation, particularly where agricultural residue is available locally and grid access is limited or unreliable.

What is the climate benefit of SPX Power™?

It displaces diesel generation and reduces transmission losses associated with centralised grid power, by generating electricity closer to where the biomass and the demand both exist.

Can SPX Power™ be combined with other SPX™ pathways?

Yes. Biomass processing can be configured to generate electricity alongside other outputs such as biochar, bio-oil or hydrogen, depending on project design.

How can I deploy an SPX Power™ project?

Apply as a Plant & Project Partner through the Partners page. Soil Passport provides technology, project development support and the digital platform; you provide land, biomass access and local operations.
