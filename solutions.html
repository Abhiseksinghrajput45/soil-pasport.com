FAQ

Solutions questions, answered.

What's the difference between a Solutions engagement and a Partnership?

Solutions covers how you work with us on a specific project or need: equipment hire, SaaS access, project implementation or consulting. Partnership (see the Partners page) is a longer-term, structured relationship, such as an SPX™ regional plant, technology licence or joint venture, with its own commercial terms.

Can I hire soil testing or weather station equipment without buying it outright?

Yes. Our partner network supports device hire and rental for both the soil testing device and the automatic weather station, paired with the Soil Passport™ app for instant digital reports, useful for farms and projects that don't want to own hardware outright.

How long does a typical project implementation take?

It depends on project scope. A technology licence or SaaS deployment can be live in weeks, while a full SPX™ plant project (assessment through commissioning) typically spans several months. We scope a timeline during project assessment, the first step of implementation.

Do I need to choose an SPX™ technology before starting a project?

No. Technology matching is one of the steps in our project implementation process. We help identify the suitable SPX™ pathway (biochar, bio-oil, bio-tar, hydrogen or power) based on your feedstock, site and goals, rather than requiring you to decide upfront.

What is Soil Passport™ SaaS?

A subscription model giving access to the digital soil, biomass and carbon intelligence platform, without needing a technology licence, project development support or hardware. Suited to organisations that want the data layer on its own.

Can governments or institutions use Soil Passport™ solutions?

Yes. Governments, NGOs and institutions can use our digital infrastructure for district-level soil mapping, farmer registration, biomass management and climate-impact monitoring at programme scale.

How do I get started?

Talk to our team through the contact form, outlining your organisation type and what you're looking to achieve: equipment hire, a SaaS subscription, project implementation or full consulting support. We'll recommend the right engagement model from there.
