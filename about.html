Our Vision

One connected ecosystem, not separate activities.

Agriculture generates enormous quantities of biomass residues, while farmers need better information about soil, water, crops and farm productivity. At the same time, industries are looking for traceable renewable materials, energy and lower-carbon alternatives. Soil Passport™ connects these challenges into one ecosystem.

  • Soil
  • Farm
  • Biomass
  • Conversion
  • BioProducts
  • Carbon
  • Soil

The objective is to create a continuous information and value cycle rather than treating soil testing, biomass management, bioenergy and carbon as separate activities.

What We Do

Four core capabilities.

1 — Soil Intelligence

A digital record of every field.

We help create a digital record of the condition of a field through soil testing, laboratory data, sensors, satellite information and other relevant datasets, bringing together pH, EC, NPK, soil organic carbon, moisture, temperature, salinity, crop and field information, and historical measurements into a baseline that can be monitored over time.

2 — Farm & Biomass Intelligence

Connecting the farm to its residues.

The platform can record the path from farmer to field, crop, residue, collection and processing, helping participating biomass plants understand where biomass is available, what type, expected quantities, collection requirements, supply-chain traceability and farmer participation.

3 — Biomass-to-Value Technology

Converting biomass into multiple products.

Through the SPX™ technology ecosystem, biomass can be processed into multiple potential products and energy streams: SPX-BIOCHAR™, SPX-BIO-OIL™, SPX-BIO-TAR™, SPX-BIO-HYDROGEN™, SPX-SYNGAS™ and renewable electricity. Exact products and yields depend on feedstock, process technology and plant configuration.

4 — Carbon & Impact Intelligence

Measuring before, during and after conversion.

Producing a bio-based product is only part of the journey. The platform can support measurement, reporting and verification (MRV), biomass and product traceability, soil carbon data, product carbon-footprint information, biochar application records, environmental data and impact monitoring, alongside recognised methodologies and independent verification where carbon credits are pursued.

Our Technology Philosophy

Trusted data connecting physical infrastructure.

We believe the future of the circular bioeconomy will require more than physical infrastructure. It will require trusted data connecting physical infrastructure. That's why Soil Passport is designed around:

Our Role in the Ecosystem

Infrastructure, not ownership.

Soil Passport does not need to own every farm or every biomass plant. Our role is to provide the technology, digital infrastructure, standards, data architecture and implementation support that allow partners to build and operate their own projects.
