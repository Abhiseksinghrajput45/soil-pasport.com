Platform

Your soil. Your data. Your climate asset.

Soil Passport™ is an agricultural intelligence platform designed to create a digital profile for agricultural land, bringing soil testing, AI, satellite intelligence, carbon and biomass data into one evolving record.

02 — Soil Passport™

An evolving digital intelligence record for every field.

It brings together soil testing, soil intelligence, AI predictions, satellite intelligence, soil carbon intelligence, crop and biomass intelligence, digital soil records, carbon/MRV data, farmer insights, and climate-action opportunities.

A farmer doesn't just receive a soil report. They receive an evolving digital intelligence record for their land.

04 — AI Soil Intelligence

Turning soil data into decisions.

Traditional soil testing tells you what is in the soil. Soil Passport™ is being built to answer what it means, what could happen next, what we should do, and how to measure the result.

  • Soil Data
  • Satellite Data
  • Crop Data
  • Climate Data
  • Historical Data
  • Field Data

Combined, these sources create a continuously evolving agricultural intelligence system.

05 — Soil Carbon Intelligence

Measure the carbon story of the land.

Soil carbon is becoming an increasingly important component of sustainable agriculture and climate action. Soil Passport™ is designed to provide a digital infrastructure for soil carbon data, baseline measurements, monitoring, field-level records, change tracking, MRV workflows, carbon project data, farmer participation and climate reporting.

  • Monitor
  • Report

06 — Biomass Intelligence

Waste is not waste.

Agricultural residues can become a resource. Soil Passport™ can connect agricultural intelligence with biomass intelligence, identifying what biomass exists, where it's located, how much is available, when, who produces it, how it can be collected, and what it can become.
