FAQ

Industry questions, answered.

Which industries can use Soil Passport™ and SPX™ technologies?

Agriculture & agribusiness, energy & power, industrial manufacturing, refining & fuels, construction & materials, forestry & land management, waste & circular economy, and hydrogen & clean fuels. Each industry is matched to specific SPX™ products as either a core or a potential application. See the matrix above.

Does every SPX™ product work for every industry?

No. We deliberately avoid claiming universal application. Each SPX™ product (BioChar™, BioOil™, BioTar™, BioH₂™, Power™ and Carbon™) is mapped only to the industries and use cases it is actually suited for, marked as either a core, established use case or a potential application pending further validation.

Can SPX BioChar™ be used in agriculture?

Yes. SPX BioChar™ is a core application for agriculture, used as a soil amendment alongside soil carbon projects, crop-residue management and biomass aggregation across farms and FPOs.

Can SPX technologies be used in construction and building materials?

SPX BioChar™ has core applications in construction, including biochar-based materials and carbon-storing materials. SPX BioTar™ has potential applications in asphalt and bitumen-related research and construction composites, subject to product testing and specification.

Are SPX BioOil™ and SPX BioTar™ suitable for refining and industrial fuels?

They have potential applications as renewable feedstocks, refining intermediates and low-carbon fuel pathways. We mark refining and fuels applications as potential rather than core because they depend on technical qualification for the specific refinery or process involved.

How does Soil Passport™ decide what counts as a core vs. a potential industry application?

A core application is an established, primary use case supported by existing deployment or well-understood technical fit. A potential application is one we see as technically relevant but not yet validated, tested or qualified for a specific industry, process or customer. We don't present an application as proven until it is.

Can waste management and circular economy projects use SPX™ technologies?

Yes. Waste & circular economy is a core-application industry for SPX BioChar™, SPX BioOil™ and SPX BioTar™, covering agricultural waste valorisation, organic residue processing, waste-to-value projects and resource recovery.
