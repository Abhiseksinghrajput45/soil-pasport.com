FAQ

SPX Carbon™ questions, answered.

What is SPX Carbon™?

SPX Carbon™ is Soil Passport's digital infrastructure for measuring, reporting and verifying (MRV) carbon outcomes, connecting soil, biomass and process-level carbon data into project-ready records.

What data does SPX Carbon™ use?

Field and process-level carbon data from soil measurements, biomass tracking and conversion technology outputs, such as biochar production.

Does SPX Carbon™ issue carbon credits?

No. SPX Carbon™ provides the MRV data layer that supports carbon project development. It works alongside independent carbon standards and registries, which handle methodology approval and credit issuance, rather than replacing them.

What kinds of carbon projects can SPX Carbon™ support?

Soil carbon projects and biochar-based carbon-removal projects are the primary use cases today, with the digital MRV workflow designed to extend to other SPX™ pathways as they're qualified.

How does SPX Carbon™ relate to Soil Passport™ intelligence?

It draws on the same soil, satellite and biomass data captured through Soil Passport™, turning that data into structured MRV records rather than requiring a separate measurement system.

How can I use SPX Carbon™ for my project?

Contact our team or apply through the Partners page to discuss project-level MRV support and carbon project development.
