FAQ

Partnership questions, answered.

Who can become a Soil Passport™ partner?

Agricultural/FPO organisations, plant and project developers, technology companies, enterprises, and investors. There are five entry points (Agricultural & FPO, Plant & Project, Technology, Enterprise, and Investment & Project Partners), so you can join at whichever point fits your organisation.

What are the different partnership models?

Four structures: Technology Licence (for organisations with existing project capability), Project Development (for organisations that need help establishing a plant), Platform Partnership (Soil Passport™ across an existing farmer or plant network), and Strategic Project / Joint Venture (for large-scale projects with shared capital and resources).

Do I need to invest capital to become a partner?

Not necessarily. A Technology Partner or Agricultural/FPO Partner typically contributes expertise, data integration or farmer access rather than capital. Capital-intensive roles, such as a Plant & Project or Investment & Project Partner establishing a regional facility, are structured separately, with terms agreed per project.

What does Soil Passport™ provide, and what does the partner provide?

Soil Passport™ provides technology, project development support, SPX™ product standards, the digital platform, the Digital Product Passport, MRV and technical support. A regional plant partner typically provides capital, land, biomass, local operations, permits, workforce and customer relationships.

How are commercial terms and royalty rates determined?

We don't publish exact commercial percentages or royalty rates on the website. Terms are structured according to project size, technology scope, investment contribution and partnership model. Submit the partner application below and we'll follow up to discuss what fits your organisation.

Can I deploy an SPX™ plant in my region?

Yes, this is the Plant & Project Partner route. Soil Passport™ provides the technology (SPX-BIOCHAR™, SPX-BIO-OIL™, SPX-BIO-TAR™, SPX-BIO-HYDROGEN™ and related pathways), project development support, plant setup support, the digital platform, product standards, MRV and technical support for your regional deployment.

How do I apply to become a partner?

Complete the Partner Application form on this page with your organisation type, project profile and area of interest. Our team will follow up to discuss the partnership model and commercial terms that fit your organisation.
