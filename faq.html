Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions.

Does Soil Passport issue carbon credits?

No. Soil Passport provides the data and records. Credits are issued by independent registries.

Who is it for?

Farmers, agribusinesses, project developers, and partners who need trusted soil and carbon records.

How do I get started?

Use the contact form on the home page and tell us about your land or project.
